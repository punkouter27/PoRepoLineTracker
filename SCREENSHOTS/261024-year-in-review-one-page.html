Year in Review on one page (2026-10-24)

  • Before: a carousel, one figure per slide behind nine dots. After: every figure on one screen.
  • A hero banner holds the year's commits over the 3D city (text on the left, the city shifted right). A bento grid of glass tiles below: lines written (wide), longest streak with its flame, active days, busiest month with the monthly bars (2×2), peak hour, biggest day, most active repository, on the rise. The shareable card and Download PNG stay at the bottom.
  • On load the tiles cascade in, counts roll up from zero on odometer reels, and 1.6 s later fireworks burst over the banner while the 12-note year chord plays (with sound on). Hovering a tile about a day or month flies the camera there.
  • With reduced motion on (for example Windows "Animation effects" off), the city used to be skipped entirely, which is why the earlier screenshot showed only the gradient. It is now drawn as a still picture.
  • Captured with Playwright against https://localhost:5001 on three seeded repositories.
